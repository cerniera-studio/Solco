Why each piece is there

The rule for all five

The Fold8 Ultra's cover (1,080 × 2,520) and main screen (2,256 × 2,504) share one pixel density, and the cover is almost exactly the main screen's right half. Every scene is one world drawn at that scale. The cover is the half you hold; the door's inside is the half you never saw. Unfolding adds information, it never zooms.

  • Membrane: a touch on one half can move something on the other. The world is one surface.
  • Concentrato: a fast fold shortens every arrival by a third and cuts its ornament to 25%. The trajectory stays.
  • Giulietta: the scenes carry no controls. Settings live in the app.
  • Battery: each scene draws only on a fold, a touch, or a minute's change of light.

Catenaria

Gaudí found his arches by hanging weighted chains and turning the model over in a mirror. Folded, the chains hang from a rod. Unfolded, the door is the mirror: the same curves, rotated about the crease, stand up as arches with spires where the weights hung. Gravity draws every line. Chains are soft, so they sway and settle; stone is rigid, so arches build from their feet with one crisp curve and never bounce. One climax: the keystone of the tallest arch lights. Pluck a chain and its twin arch lights along the load path. Touch an arch and its chain answers.

Stagioni

From the tree in the first recording. The trunk rises from the hinge. Folded, the canopy shows the season of today's date. Unfolded, the canopy runs back through the year towards the far edge: summer by the crease, then spring blossom, then winter frost. The leaves bloom outwards from the crease, nearest first. Folding shakes a few loose autumn leaves down, and they stay where they land. Brush the canopy and the leaves lean away; loose ones fall.

Ponte

A bascule bridge pivots on its hinge, so the crease is the pivot. Folded, the road ends at a raised span with its red navigation lamp lit. Unfolded, the span has come down across the river and seats with one click, then the lamps and windows of a Bauhaus town light outwards from the crease, one running head at a time. Steel is rigid: no bounce. Touch the river and the reflections wobble, then settle.

Fanale

Folded, a red ring hangs over a wet road like a moon, its bar of beads pointing at the hinge. Unfolded, its twin lights bead by bead and both bloom once: they were tail lamps all along. No badge, no serpent: the light carries the brand. Tap to lock and the lamps flash twice.

Goccia

Folded, a drop forms on a leaf tip, swelling a touch past its shape and settling (water is soft). Unfolded, the hinge's magnetic settle jolts the leaf, the drop falls the length of the screen and splashes into the pond the door was hiding. That splash is the one climax. Fold again and a new drop forms. Touch the water for a ring.

Left out

Pearls, sparkles and drifting motion at rest, because no force puts them there. A clock or date on the scenes, because the lock screen has one. Copies of the recordings' own scenes (the sandcastle, the golf dog, the lighthouse): the device's idea is taken, their pictures are not.
